FAQs

Questions?

Find answers to common questions about Founder Finance. Can’t find what you’re looking for? Feel free to contact us.

Why make an open source Trello?

When Trello launched in 2011, it blew everyone away with its carefully designed simplicity, but over the years it lost its magic and grew into something closer to “Jira Lite”. This project is our attempt to recapture the original magic of Trello’s simplicity, in open source.

What’s the difference between Founder Finance and Trello?

The main difference between Founder Finance and Trello is that Founder Finance is open source, allowing anyone to view, modify, and contribute to our code. Our cloud offering also offers no restrictions on features for individual use, whereas Trello locks basic features such as the number of boards you can create behind a paywall.

How is the project funded?

For long-term sustainability, we recognise all good open source projects need a source of revenue. Ours comes from the paid cloud offering for workspaces with multiple users and for custom workspace slugs. There’s no obligation to use it, and you can self-host the software on your own infrastructure free of charge.

How do I import my Trello boards?

Importing your Trello boards into Founder Finance is easy. You can follow our step-by-step guide here.

How do I get a custom URL?

You can get a custom workspace URL, like founderfinance.app/acme, by going into your workspace settings and upgrading to a Team workspace subscription. All subscriptions help fund the development of the project!

Do you offer a free plan?

Yes, we offer a forever free plan for individual use. No restrictions, no paywalls, no limits.

What license are you using?

We are using the AGPL-3.0 license.

How do I invite team members?

You can invite team members by clicking the “Invite” button in the top right corner of the members page and entering their email address. They will receive an email with a link to join the workspace.

How do I self-host?

You can self-host by following the instructions in our repo.
